# Sources

Every template in this repo, where it comes from and why it is in. All sources were retrieved on 18 November 2026.

## Criteria

- Drafted by a committee of practitioners or issued by a regulator, and widely used; or drafted by one recognised firm, under an open license.
- B2B contracts only, from a named publisher, under a license that allows republishing. At most three per contract type.

The text of each template is the publisher's, converted to Markdown. Only the layout is harmonised: headings, numbering, highlighted fill-in fields, options, drafting notes and signature blocks; page numbers and footers are dropped. Common Paper, oneNDA, oneSaaS, General Legal, YC and MCC-AI files are converted from the publisher's Word file; EU texts from the Official Journal; Bonterms files from Bonterms' GitHub repositories, except the Software License Terms, which come from bonterms.com.
