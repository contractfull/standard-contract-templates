# AI addendum and AI clauses

- **Use when** a product includes AI, or you buy an AI system and need terms on training data, outputs, transparency and oversight.
- **Don't use when** you need a full contract: all three are added to a main agreement.

**Not sure which?**

- Private B2B deal → Common Paper.
- EU public procurement → MCC-AI, high-risk or light.
